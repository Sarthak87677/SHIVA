Controls

Hand gestures

✋ Open palmmove hand left/right/up/down to rotate the focused 3-D view
🤏 Pinchraise / lower the pinched hand to zoom in / out
✊ Fist (hold)play / pause the universe
✌️ Two fingers (hold)switch Evolved / Newtonian / side by side
3️⃣ Three fingers (hold)move focus between universe and prototype viewer
☝️ Point + swipenext / previous prototype · scrub time in the universe

Hand tracking runs locally in your browser (MediaPipe); no video leaves your computer. The first start downloads the model (~18 MB) and needs internet.
