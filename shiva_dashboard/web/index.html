Control SHIVA-1 with your hand

Your webcam tracks one or two hands right here in the browser (nothing is uploaded). A glowing cursor follows your hand — every button, list and 3-D view on the page works with it.

☝️Move your handthe cursor follows
🤏Pinchclick · hold and move to drag
✋Open palmrotate the universe
🙌Two handspull apart to zoom in
✊Fist (hold)play / pause
✌️Two fingers (hold)next universe view
✦Sandboxyour hand becomes a star that pulls the particles
🤙Little finger (hold)write in the air on your camera view (👍 works too)

Needs a webcam and internet the first time (the hand model is ~18 MB). Works best in Chrome or Edge, with your hand 40–80 cm from the camera and the room reasonably lit.

Controls

Hand gestures

☝️ Move your handa cursor follows it across the whole page
🤏 Quick pinchclick whatever is under the cursor (buttons, prototypes, figures, the time bar)
🤏 Pinch, hold & movedrag: rotate a 3-D view, move the time bar, scroll a list or the page
✋ Open palmrotate the 3-D view under the cursor
🙌 Two open handsmove them apart / together to zoom in / out
3️⃣ Three fingersmove up / down to zoom (one-handed)
✊ Fist (hold)play / pause
✌️ Two fingers (hold)next universe view: evolved → Newtonian → side by side → sandbox
✦ Sandboxyour hand is a gold star pulling the particles; pinch to make it 4× heavier
🤙 Little finger up (hold) · 👍 thumbs up (hold)air writing on / off
In air writing — ☝️ index finger only = write · ✌️ / ✋ = pen up · hover a button ~0.6 s or pinch it = select colour, size, pen style, eraser, text font · ✊ fist = rub out · Undo / Redo / Clear / Save PNG in the bottom bar

Hand tracking runs locally in your browser (MediaPipe); no video leaves your computer. The first start downloads the model (~18 MB) and needs internet.
